# AlmaLinux Board of Directors Election 2026 - Cohort A

## Process

The AlmaLinux OS Foundation is running an election to fill 2 Cohort A seats on the Board of Directors. The election will start on September 14th, 2026.

At this stage we are looking for nominees for the Board of Director positions. To be nominated, a candidate must:

- Be an AlmaLinux OS Foundation member in good standing by July 16th, 2026
- Not be a member of the Election Committee running this election

Nominations come from members in good standing: Individual and Mirror members can each nominate one candidate other than themselves, and Sponsor members (Platinum, Gold, Ruby, Silver) can nominate a representative affiliated with their organization.

To nominate someone, send the name and e-mail address of your nominee to [elections@almalinux.org](mailto:elections@almalinux.org) from your e-mail address registered in [AlmaLinux Accounts](https://accounts.almalinux.org). Nominations are open from August 3rd to September 8th, 2026.

Please check the [FAQ section](#faq) for more information.

### FAQ

- When does the election start?
  - The election starts on September 14, 2026 and runs until October 2, 2026. If we don't reach quorum by October 2nd, the election will stay open until quorum is reached.
  - To reach the quorum we need at least 10% of all eligible Foundation members to vote. Because each membership class (Individual, Silver Sponsor, etc.) carries a different voting weight, the members who vote must also add up to at least 10% of the total voting weight.

- Who can vote?
  - Any Platinum, Gold, Ruby, Silver, Individual, or Mirror member in good standing, approved by the Membership Committee before July 16th, 2026.
  - A member is in good standing when they follow the Foundation's rules - the Certificate of Incorporation, the bylaws, the membership application, and any policies adopted by the board - and have paid any applicable sponsorship fees on time. Individual and Mirror membership is free.

- How can I vote?
  - If you are eligible to vote, you will receive an email with the link to the voting page on [AlmaLinux Accounts](https://accounts.almalinux.org/elections/), and your voting credentials, on the day the election starts.

- How many votes does each member get?
  - You cast a single ranked ballot. Depending on your membership class, that ballot carries a set weight:
    - Platinum sponsors: 50
    - Gold sponsors: 15
    - Ruby sponsors: 5
    - Silver sponsors: 5
    - Individual and Mirror members: 1

- How are votes tallied?
  - Voting is ranked choice: instead of picking a single name, you rank the candidates in your order of preference. Your ballot carries the number of votes for your membership class (see above).
  - Winners are selected using [Meek Single Transferable Vote (High-Precision Variant)](https://accounts.almalinux.org/elections/algorithm/), a ranked-choice method for filling multiple seats. Each seat has a target threshold (a quota). Your vote starts with your top choice; when a candidate has more than they need to reach the quota, the surplus transfers fractionally to voters' next choices, and candidates with the fewest votes are eliminated with their votes transferring onward. This repeats until every seat is filled.
  - The tally is deterministic and produces a public audit trail, so anyone can independently verify the result from the published ballots ledger and audit log once the election concludes.

- Who can be nominated?
  - Any member in good standing as of the eligibility cutoff (July 16th, 2026).
  - Members of the [Election Committee](#election-committee) cannot be nominated in the election they are running.

- Can I nominate myself?
  - No. As an Individual or Mirror member, you can nominate one candidate other than yourself, and that candidate must be a member in good standing.
  - The exception is if you're the representative of a Sponsor member (Platinum, Gold, Ruby, or Silver), in which case you can nominate yourself as your organization's nominee.

- How can I nominate someone?
  - From your e-mail address registered in [AlmaLinux Accounts](https://accounts.almalinux.org), send an e-mail to [elections@almalinux.org](mailto:elections@almalinux.org) with the name and e-mail address of your nominee.

- My company is a Sponsor member, can it nominate any company employee?
  - Yes, Sponsors can nominate any company employee, but if the employee is terminated, or company sponsorship lapses, the person may be asked to step down from the role.
  - Sponsor members can only nominate a candidate affiliated with the sponsoring organization.

- How many seats are on the Board, and how many are vacant?
  - The Board is composed of 8 seats divided into four cohorts (A, B, C, and D), and one cohort comes up for election each year. This election is for Cohort A, which has 2 seats.

- How long does a Director serve?
  - A term on the Board is 4 years, unless the Director leaves earlier. One of the four cohorts is up for election each year.

- What platform will you use for the elections?
  - The election will be held on [AlmaLinux Accounts](https://accounts.almalinux.org/elections/).

- How are vacancies handled in the event that a Director leaves before the end of their term?
  - If a seat is vacated less than three months before the next election, the Board may fill the vacancy by a majority vote of the Directors, or may leave it open until that election.
  - Otherwise, the vacancy is filled by a vote of the members. In either case, whoever fills the seat serves the remainder of that cohort's term.

- Are Directors on the Board compensated?
  - Board positions are not compensated positions.

- Is Director at the AlmaLinux OS Foundation a full-time position?
  - It is not a full-time position.

- How is the Chair of the Board of Directors elected?
  - The Chair is one of the officers elected by the Directors at the first meeting of a new Board of Directors, or as needed.

- Can two members of the Board also work at the same company?
  - No.

- Where can I find more information?
  - In the [AlmaLinux OS Foundation Bylaws](https://almalinux.org/p/foundation-bylaws/).
